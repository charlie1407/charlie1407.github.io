About

An eye for art, a mind for operations

Since 2021 I've run Curio Party, a vintage visual-art gallery built around accessible collecting, pop-up exhibitions and online storytelling.

Through Curio Atelier I advise collectors and artists on choosing, presenting and living with work. I also publish an art opinion journal, The Curio Journal, where I share my views on the works, artists and exhibitions I encounter. And I plan exhibitions, bringing together the artists, collectors and venues behind each show.

My other career is in technology. I lead production services for AI platforms at a major Canadian bank, which taught me calm facilitation, clear communication with every kind of audience, and how to run things reliably when people are counting on them. I bring all of that to the art world.

I trained as an engineer at the University of Toronto, play piano, and speak English and Mandarin.

The Poetry of the Ordinary

19 Frames, 523 Parliament St, Toronto · March 1–15, 2026

Paint, textile, paper and found materials

Curio Atelier's inaugural exhibition. I paired contemporary work by eight artists with vintage pieces from Curio Party to look at everyday moments: hands held, animals watching, tables set.

Jackie Bradshaw, Stephanie Baron, Brent Hardisty, Bradley Reinhardt, Riesbri, Charlotte Sigurdson, Laura Thippawong, Amy Thomson, with vintage works by Ivor Monk, Emir A. and unsigned Japanese and Canadian artists.
